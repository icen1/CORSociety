# Roman Society 1.1.329

Bug-fix release based on Workshop reports.

## Extinct-house notification spam / slowdown
- `monthlyTick` now persists `lastProcessedMonth` immediately and runs every step
  inside its own try/catch with a `finally` save. Previously, any exception mid-pass
  meant the month was never marked as processed, so the whole pass (including the
  "House Extinguished" modal) re-ran on every game tick.
- Extinct-house popups are now opt-in (`society.settings.extinctionNotices`, off by
  default). Extinctions are still logged and archived under Dead houses.

## Praise in public / Support spam
- "Praise in public" now has a 4 month per-person cooldown (button disabled with tooltip).
- "Support spouse/family/house" has a 3 month per-person cooldown.
- Both are also enforced inside the action, not only in the UI.

## Purchased slaves missing from household
- Owned slaves are injected into the live household list (vanilla only rebuilds it
  from spouse/children links) and kept in sync; removed again when sold/freed/dead.
- `repairFalsePlayerSlaveFlags` no longer un-enslaves a character that is tracked in
  `playerSlaves`, regardless of which flags are present on the character.
