# Roman Society 1.1.330

## Family vanishing on a tick
- A house was archived as extinct whenever its Society member lists resolved to zero living
  people, even if living characters still belonged to it, so a family could disappear at random.
  `retireHouse`/`shouldRetireHouse` now also scan the real game characters
  (`houseHasLivingPeopleRaw`) and refuse to retire a house with living members or the player's dynasty.
- `repairFamilyLinkIntegrity` no longer severs spouse links that touch the player's household.
