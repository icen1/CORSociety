# Roman Society 1.1.331

## Player family absorbed by an AI cadet house (family "vanishing")
Reported from a save inspection: the player character was assigned to an AI-controlled
cadet house (`foundedReason: "npc cadet foundation"`), most children and grandchildren were
tagged to it but missing from its member list, and dead children still showed as active.

- **Root cause:** `maybeCreateNpcCadetHouses` iterated every dynasty, including the player's.
  It picked a living relative as founder and moved the whole branch (spouse + descendants,
  which includes the player) into a new AI house. `syncPlayerHouseRecord` then skipped anyone
  pointing at that cadet, so the protected player household lost its members.
- **Fix:** the player's dynasty is never split by NPC cadet foundation (`maybeCreateNpcCadetHouses`
  and `createCadetHouse` both refuse). Player-initiated branches are unaffected.
- **Save repair:** new `repairPlayerFamilyHouseAssignment` runs every month and whenever the
  player house is synced. It moves everyone in the player's dynasty back to the player house,
  dissolves leftover NPC-founded cadets of that dynasty, and drops dead characters from active
  member lists (kept under dead members).
