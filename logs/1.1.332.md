# Roman Society 1.1.332

Everything since 1.1.331.

## Gameplay
- **The marriage screen offers three separate choices**, where the game had one checkbox that
  did two things:
  - a matrilineal match;
  - giving the child's share of the inheritance now, offered to every household descendant
    (the child can stay at home, and the split at your death leaves them out);
  - starting their own household.
  A child whose children would take the spouse's name joins the spouse's household instead.
- **A trial you can fight.** When you or someone of your household is accused, the court sits
  two months later, and a trial event offers the defence:
  - a plea by anyone of the household who can speak in court (a judicial orator or a lawyer;
    the accused can plead their own case), adding 1.5% per point of the speaker's eloquence,
    up to 90%;
  - bribing the jurors (three times the fine), +24%;
  - a public defence paid in influence by your station, +16%;
  - leaving it to the court.
  A bribe or a public defence is capped at 75%, the court alone at 55%. NPCs are judged at once.
- **The Society's politics count in the game.**
  - Standing for office goes through the game's own elections: months 10-12, its
    prerequisites, the real job and the former-office traits.
  - Buying ludi seats for the voters in the game's campaign is recorded as hidden ambitus, and
    the ticket choices now say so.
  - Dictator and emperor get traits, a badge, and monthly influence and prestige.
  - Legions and clients pay monthly influence, and legions also prestige.
  - Campaigns are fought in the game's wars and earn the veteran trait; triumphs earn the
    corona triumphalis, and acclamation an Imperator trait.
  - Cognomina show in the name, adoption is a real adoption, and priesthoods are real jobs.
- **Political prices follow the economy**, not your purse: cash costs scale with the median
  wealth of the NPC houses (0.5x-4x), and every label shows the price you will pay.
- **Women of your gens carry no cognomen**, as Roman women of the Republic had none: "Tullia
  Tertia", not the game's "Tullia Cica Tertia" for a Tullius Cicero.
- **Branches of your dynasty.**
  - Only a real relative founds an NPC branch: someone whose parents lead, through game
    characters, up to you or one of your ancestors.
  - The relatives the mod invents for your tree never do, nor does the unlinked man the game
    gives a new player, nor their lines.
  - You, your spouse, descendants, ancestors and household never move into an NPC branch, and
    your house always heads your dynasty.
  - A dynasty's name follows the family name you chose in the game, and its branches are
    renamed with it.
- **NPC property feuds really move a property** between the two houses and sour their
  relations; before, the log reported seizures that never stuck.
- **Play As is offered on the living members of your household**, and Attempt Murder has a
  small icon.

## Fixes
- **"Move out?" could take the whole family fortune.** The game's share calculator falls back
  to the whole estate when it cannot reach the character, for example a disinherited child or a
  child of one. Such a character now moves out with nothing paid, and the marriage screen never
  offers them a share.
- **A child given their share early is no longer offered "Restore Inheritance"** (a second share
  at your death).
- **Your own trials were never judged.** The stale-state clean-up dropped a pending trial on the
  day the court sat, before the verdict.
- **Statuses the Society took away stayed on screen.** The game hides a status only by its
  `active` flag, which its own `setCharacterStatusActive` never touches, so a released prisoner
  showed "Imprisoned ... until 532-4" years later, and freed slaves kept their slave status.
  They are now removed, and stale ones are cleared from older saves.
- **NPC families no longer hop from house to house.**
  - Before: a founder took the whole family into a new branch, the old house died out, and he
    founded again, so the same branch was founded twice and each new house started with a
    third of the old one's wealth.
  - Now someone always stays behind, and nobody founds a second house.
  - A dynasty whose origin house died out is headed by its strongest branch.
- **Brides keep their own gens.** A generated bride living in your house could be moved into
  your gens, and her children were born with an adoption agnomen of their own gens
  ("Marcus Tullius Cicero "Tullianus"").
- **NPCs no longer die the month the game first checks them.**
  - This killed the husbands of your daughters and granddaughters within weeks of the
    wedding, and sent the widows home.
  - The game scales NPC death risk by its character count, and the mod's thousands of dead ghost
    ancestors inflated that count about tenfold.
  - The ghosts stay in the save but no longer count, so NPCs die at about vanilla rates again
    and NPC births recover.
- **The Roman Society no longer dies out.** Houses could never gain new members (the generation
  limits counted everyone ever generated, the dead included), so they aged down to one member
  and went extinct. The limits now count the living.
- **Houses no longer lose track of their own members**, so they no longer look empty and are
  refilled again. Members are found by the house they belong to.
- **NPC houses no longer duplicate money.**
  - A house selling property above its limit was paid but kept the property, every turn, so a
    shrunken house could grow to millions in cash and strength.
  - Trade deals and support from such a house then paid absurd sums, and the excess-cash events
    (Greed / Theft / Ransacked) kept firing on you.
  - Purchases by houses were also lost.
- **White screens / crash on load.**
  - Slaves bought by NPC cadet houses were given an invalid dynasty, and a game event crashed
    on them.
  - The game then logged a full copy of its state into the save (13 MB per error) until saving
    ran out of memory.
  - Slaves now get the right dynasty, and the mod strips those state copies from the game's log
    before they are saved.
- NPC houses never buy members of your family as slaves.
- NPC simulation no longer marries off or impregnates you, your spouse, descendants or
  descendants' spouses, never generates filler relatives into your house, and your house never
  commits NPC feud murders.
- Houses no longer show as "undefined" ("Members of undefined", "Head house: undefined").
- Disabled Society options show their reason instead of an empty box, and the
  `t.forEach is not a function` render errors are gone.
- Back on a house opened from Allies and Patrons (or Rivals, or a dynasty) returns there again
  after acting on that house.
- Household rites, buying slaves, buying or capturing an enslaved person and legitimizing a
  slave's child no longer take the money and then refuse when you have only 1-2x the cost. Any
  refusal refunds what the game charged on the click.
- After reloading a save from the same month, NPC houses and Society menus use the reloaded
  save's data.
- The debt-sale loan button and the tax-fraud event keep working after loading a save.
- One failing Roman system no longer silently skips the others that month.
- The imperial inquiry no longer charges influence without a target.
- The debug console no longer copies whole snapshots into the game's saved session log.
- Overlay timers no longer throw "daapi is not defined" while a save loads.

## Performance
- **No more freeze when a month starts, and no stutter every few seconds while you idle.**
  - The mod reads the game state live instead of copying it.
  - It skips writes that would change nothing.
  - It lets the game refresh its characters once a month instead of hundreds of times.
  - It runs its own month in short slices after the game's month step.
  - In a test world the mod's time per month fell from 2.6 s to under 0.1 s.
- **The game state is several times smaller** (12.2 MB -> 3.4 MB in a real save, smaller again
  with the new icons).
  - Every month advance, autosave and Society screen handles far less data, and the save file
    shrinks to match.
  - Dead characters no longer carry the Murder / Play As buttons.
  - Once a game year, at a month advance, the dead nobody alive descends from are deleted. Your
    family, every ancestor of anyone alive, extinct houses in the archive and anyone still
    referenced are always kept.
- **A month profiler:** `window.corSocietyProfile = true`, a few months, then `corSocietyPerf()`
  in the command console. Each change has a switch that restores the older behaviour:
  `corSocietyDisableLiveState`, `corSocietyDisableNoopFilter`,
  `corSocietyDisableRefreshCoalescing`, `corSocietyDisableMonthlySlicing`
  (`corSocietyMonthlySliceMs` sets the slice), `corSocietyDisableSnapshotCache` and
  `corSocietyDisableDeadButtonStrip`.

## Save compatibility
Saves from 1.1.331 load and are repaired once, on the first load (migration steps 3-8):
- Characters with an invalid dynasty are repointed to their house's real dynasty (also checked
  monthly).
- Houses inflated by the money duplication, every house except yours, lose the glitch property
  and the duplicated cash, and are brought back to the richest honest house of their order.
  One-way.
- Nameless houses are named.
- Branches of your dynasty that no real relative founded are folded back into your house.
  One-way.
- Hidden ambitus records from ludi tickets bought before the warning existed are wiped. One-way.
- Brides moved into your gens return to their own, and their children lose the false adoption
  agnomen. One-way.

Each month the Society also sweeps stale Play As and Murder buttons and stale statuses, and gives
the women of your gens their empty cognomen. The version change re-runs the version-keyed
repairs once, so the first month and the first menu after the update are slower.
